THE 6-STAGE STUDIO PROTOCOL

Every vehicle undergoes our clinical white-glove intake and surgical installation process.

01 / STAGE

Surface Decon

Multi-chemical iron fallout bath and clay bar treatment removing sub-surface brake contamination.

02 / STAGE

Paint Correction

2-to-3 stage rotary compounding eliminating 98% of swirl marks under 5000K high-CRI inspection lights.

03 / STAGE

Laser PPF Plot

Bespoke CAD laser templating to ensure zero razor-to-paint blade contact across edges and emblems.

Micro-edge shaved heat shrinking across double-curved hypercar glass in a positive-pressure cleanroom.

05 / STAGE

06 / STAGE
